Root causes addressed in the integration:
1. Heading and laptop are one sticky composition, so Desktop Console text never
   drifts away while the lid is opening.
2. Scroll is remapped into three phases instead of mapping the whole video linearly:
   closed hold -> lid opening -> open settle.
3. The showcase ancestor uses overflow: clip instead of overflow: hidden so it
   does not create the scrolling ancestor that breaks sticky positioning.
4. Only the real lid-motion part of the approved asset is scrubbed.
5. Dashboard click is disabled until the laptop is fully open.
6. Compact/mobile and prefers-reduced-motion layouts show the open state without
   scroll scrubbing.
7. Existing static laptop remains as codec/media failure fallback.
