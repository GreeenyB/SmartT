## Runtime/offline parity

Both use `resample_window`, preserve raw missingness, require a full 16.0s window and 85% coverage, and distinguish GPS data freshness from speed freshness.

## What ML is allowed to do today

Raw behavior probabilities, UNKNOWN abstention (validation-selected threshold `0.85`), anomaly score, and shadow logging only.

## Physical readiness / remaining validation

Physical baseline/event/recovery collection is required before any alert authority, calibration claim, field false-alert claim, or deep-learning investigation. There is no evidence that a CNN solves a remaining temporal confusion beyond engineered features.
